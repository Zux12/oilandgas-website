A ratiometric fluorescent biosensor based on magnetic-assisted hybridization chain reaction and DNA-templated silver nanoclusters for sensitive microRNA detection

Zheng Wei Wong, Kasturi Muthoosamy, Nur Aliana Hidayah Mohamed, Siu Yee New

Published on Oil & Gas: 2026-11-10

Shared by Oil & Gas Editorial

Summary

The authors use a ratio of fluorescence signals to detect microRNA. Their biosensor combines a magnetic-assisted hybridization chain reaction with silver nanoclusters formed on DNA templates.
