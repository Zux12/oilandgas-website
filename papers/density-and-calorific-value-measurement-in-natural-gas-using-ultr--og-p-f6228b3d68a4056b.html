Density and calorific value measurement in natural gas using ultrasonic flow meters

Kjell-Eivind Frøysa, Per Lunde

Shared by Oil & Gas Editorial

Summary

2005. The authors examine how an ultrasonic meter can help determine natural gas density and calorific value. These properties connect measured gas volume to the mass or energy used in commercial settlements. See paper 5 in the workshop proceedings.
