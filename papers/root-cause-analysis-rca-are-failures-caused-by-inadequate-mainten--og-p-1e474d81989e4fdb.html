Root Cause Analysis (RCA): Are Failures Caused by Inadequate Maintenance?

Muhamed Jamil Khan Nurul Amin, Noor Syafynas Yusof, Ahmad Azim Pauzi, Kok Ping Hun, Nor Azizy Suratanin, Za’im Zainal, Nurul Aizad M Safan, M Anif Adenan, Ahmad Aiman Mohamad, Jayakumar Viswanathan

Shared by Oil & Gas Editorial

Summary

Platform: A Journal of Engineering (2021). This paper examines whether inadequate maintenance explains equipment failures and declining reliability. It encourages engineers to investigate failure causes objectively rather than assume that maintenance is responsible.
