Summary

Pipeline Technology Journal (Issue 2, 2021). PETRONAS engineers describe an integrated approach to pipeline integrity management that brings asset information, inspections, risk assessments and maintenance planning together. The work connects digitalisation with pipeline reliability and prevention of leaks and ruptures. The source links to the journal issue.
