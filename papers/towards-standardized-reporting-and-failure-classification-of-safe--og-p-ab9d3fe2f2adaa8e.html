Shenae Lee, Maria Vatshaug Ottermo, Stein Hauge, Mary Ann Lundteigen

Published on Oil & Gas: 2026-11-06

Process Safety and Environmental Protection (2023). Maintenance teams often describe a fault in their own words. This study examines how those descriptions can be converted into consistent failure classifications for tracking safety equipment performance.
