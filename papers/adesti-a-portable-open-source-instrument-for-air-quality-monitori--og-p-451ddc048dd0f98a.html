ADESTI: A portable open source instrument for air quality monitoring in surgical operating rooms

Prisma Megantoro, Anna Surgean Veterini, Nagesparan Ainarappan, Nayu Nurrohma Hidayat, Fikarul Mujtahida, Mochammad Dava Septiadi Pratama, Muhammad Aqil Afham Rahmat

Shared by Oil & Gas Editorial

Summary

The authors describe an open-source portable instrument for monitoring air quality in surgical operating rooms. The design offers an example of integrating sensors into a portable measurement system.
