Industry news · Sarawak, Malaysia

Partners plan joint development of Lang Lebah and B14

On 2 November, PETRONAS Carigali, PTTEP, KUFPEC and TotalEnergies announced agreements covering two gas fields offshore Sarawak. TotalEnergies would become the operator of both fields once the transactions close, subject to regulatory approval.

Engineering perspective The fields lie about 14 km apart. Developing them together gives the partners the option to share facilities. For measurement teams, one design question is where to meter each field’s output before the streams are combined.
