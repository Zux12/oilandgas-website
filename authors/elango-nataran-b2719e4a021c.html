  • Priority in Safety Management System Using Decision-Tree Technique

    Kanesan Muthusamy, Liew Meena, Elango Nataran, Victor Nagesparan

    The authors used a workforce questionnaire and a decision tree to rank factors affecting occupational safety at a manufacturing company. The study examines where managers can focus improvements to their safety programme.
