Publications

  • Optimization Through Artificial Neural Network on a Programmable Logic Controller for a Sludge Drying Plant

    M. Z. Mohamed, M. F. M. Ghazali, S. M. Idrus, N. A. Wahab

    9th IEEE International Colloquium on Signal Processing and its Applications (2013). The study explores using an artificial neural network on a programmable logic controller for sludge-drying optimisation.

  • Parameter Optimization of a Sludge Drying Plant for Processing Capability Improvement

    M. Z. Mohamed, M. F. M. Ghazali, S. M. Idrus, N. A. Wahab

    IEEE Conference on Control, Systems & Industrial Informatics, Bandung (2012). The authors use operating data to identify subsystems and parameters that affect the dryness of sludge sent for disposal.

  • Cost Effective Sludge Management Through Processing Capability Improvement of a Sludge Drying Plant

    M. Z. Mohamed, M. F. M. Ghazali, Z. Mokhtar, S. M. Idrus, N. A. Wahab

    10th Asian Control Conference (2015). The authors examine how improving sludge-drying capability can reduce disposal costs.

  • Processing Capability Improvement Through Parameter Optimization of a Sludge Drying Plant

    M. Z. Mohamed, M. F. M. Ghazali, S. M. Idrus, N. A. Wahab

    IEEE Control and System Graduate Research Colloquium, Shah Alam, paper ID101, p. 388 (2012). The proceedings list this paper on parameter selection and sludge-drying capability on page 388.

  • Maximizing Output of a Sludge Drying Plant Through Multiple Parameter Optimization

    M. Z. Mohamed, M. F. M. Ghazali, S. M. Idrus, N. A. Wahab

    8th IEEE Conference on Industrial Electronics and Applications (2013). This paper examines how several operating parameters influence the solids content of sludge cake.
