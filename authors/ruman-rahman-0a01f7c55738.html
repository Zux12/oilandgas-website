  • Amplification-free and direct fluorometric determination of telomerase activity in cell lysates using chimeric DNA-templated silver nanoclusters

    Shi Ting Lee, Ruman Rahman, Kasturi Muthoosamy, Nur Aliana Hidayah Mohamed, XiaoDi Su, Saad Tayyab, Siu Yee New

    This optical assay measures telomerase activity in cell lysates. DNA-templated silver nanoclusters provide a fluorescent readout without an amplification step.
