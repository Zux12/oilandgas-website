Publications

  • Energy Optimisation and Management System for Smart Buildings with IoT and Support Vector Regression-Based Predictive Modelling

    Nagesparan Ainarappan, Effariza Hanafi, Zhao Guangpeng, Harini Nadarajan, Norrabiatul Adawiyah Binti Jusoh

    The authors combine connected sensors and support vector regression to predict energy consumption in a commercial building and inform energy management.

  • Early Flood Monitoring and Detection System with a Machine Learning’s Neural Network Technique and IoT Platform

    Harinidevi Nadarajan, Nagesparan Ainarappan, Farah Adilah Binti Jamaludin

    The study combines water sensing, connected monitoring and a neural network for early flood detection. It illustrates how field readings can feed a remote warning system.

  • Analysis of Continuous Vibration Monitoring on Frequency Domain Using Various Sensors

    Nagesparan Ainarappan, Tham Ming Sum, Kanesan Muthusamy

    The authors monitored motor and drive vibration as speed and running time changed. They analysed successive windows of sensor data to distinguish normal operating changes from persistent vibration that warrants inspection.

  • OSH Hazards Risk Assessment Using AHP Technique – A Case Study of an Electronics Manufacturing Company

    Kanesan Muthusamy, Joses Shem John David, Shannen Darshini Surasan Nair, Nagesparan Ainarappan

    This electronics manufacturing case study uses the analytic hierarchy process to rank occupational hazards. It illustrates how a safety team can compare risks when deciding which controls to address first.
