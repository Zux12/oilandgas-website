  • Integrated Management Framework for Performance Challenges in Rural Off-Grid Microgrids: Addressing Deterioration in Electrification Systems

    Tinton Dwi Atmaja, Dalila Mat Said, Sevia Mahdaliza Idrus, Ahmad Fudholi, Ahmad Rajani, Dian Andriani, Rudi Darussalam

    A rural microgrid can lose performance when funding lapses, spare parts arrive late or local operators lack support. The authors connect these operational problems in a framework for managing off-grid electricity systems.
