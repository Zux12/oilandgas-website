Publications

  • Handbook of uncertainty calculations. Ultrasonic fiscal gas metering stations

    Per Lunde, Kjell-Eivind Frøysa

    2001. This handbook addresses uncertainty at ultrasonic fiscal gas metering stations. It gives custody-transfer engineers a station-level perspective when assessing the uncertainty of reported gas quantities.

  • Handbook of uncertainty calculations. Fiscal orifice gas and turbine oil metering stations

    Eivind O. Dahl, Kjell-Eivind Frøysa, Per Lunde

    2003. This handbook covers uncertainty calculations for two fiscal metering arrangements: orifice meters for gas and turbine meters for oil. It is relevant to engineers preparing a station uncertainty assessment.
