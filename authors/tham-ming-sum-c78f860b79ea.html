  • Analysis of Continuous Vibration Monitoring on Frequency Domain Using Various Sensors

    Nagesparan Ainarappan, Tham Ming Sum, Kanesan Muthusamy

    The authors monitored motor and drive vibration as speed and running time changed. They analysed successive windows of sensor data to distinguish normal operating changes from persistent vibration that warrants inspection.
