How selections work

Editorial evaluation

Research relevance, technical contribution, practical applicability, evidence, originality and timely value are considered. Initial automated ranking is provisional and does not represent an independent scientific review.

International participation

Publications from around the world are eligible. Research with verified Malaysian affiliations receives additional visibility when qualified candidates are closely matched.

Permanent recognition

Featured publications remain in this archive after their seven-day spotlight. This is an editorial distinction, not an accredited award or journal endorsement.
